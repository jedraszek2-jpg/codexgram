2. Who may use Codexgram

Codexgram is an invitation-only beta intended for people aged 18 or older. The app does not ask for a date of birth or technically verify age or invitations. Do not use the Service if you do not meet these requirements.

This Policy applies to the Codexgram mobile app and its current web build. It does not cover third-party services that link to or provide access to Codexgram; those services publish their own privacy information.

3. Information we handle

Depending on how you use Codexgram, we and our service providers handle:

  • Sign-in and account information: your account identifier and authentication information managed by Clerk. Sign-in is available through Google OAuth or email and password. If you choose Google, Google and Clerk process information needed to authenticate you; the exact fields returned by Google depend on your Google account and settings.
  • Profile information: username, display name, profile photo or avatar, bio, account status, and profile creation/update times. The app may initialize profile details from your Clerk account, including your username, name, and image URL.
  • Content and media: posts, captions, comments, stories, photos, videos, and text messages you submit. We also handle technical media details such as file type, size, duration when applicable, storage reference, and upload time. The app does not include code that strips image metadata before upload.
  • Social and usage information: follows, likes, comments, activity notifications, conversation membership, messages, and associated timestamps and read status.
  • Language preference: the app reads your device language setting to choose a default language and stores your selected English or Polish language locally on your device. The profile screen’s “Location” choice changes the app language; it does not save a physical location.

We do not request access to your contacts or location in the app. Photo-library access is used when you select media; camera access is used to take a story photo. Microphone permission is disabled in the app configuration. Content you select may itself contain information or image metadata you did not intend to share.

We do not collect your Clerk password in the Codexgram Convex database. We do not currently provide AI features, ads, or product analytics. Provider-generated technical logs and diagnostics are governed by provider configuration and retention, which may not be visible in the app code.

4. How and why we use information

We use the information above to create and authenticate accounts; maintain profiles; host and display content; provide feeds, search, follows, likes, comments, stories, activity and messaging; operate account deletion; protect the Service; and respond to support, legal, or privacy requests.

The following legal bases are provisional drafting assumptions and must be checked by the controller and a qualified privacy lawyer before publication:

  • Contract: account, profile, content, social, and messaging processing needed to provide the features you request.
  • Legitimate interests: maintaining and securing the beta, preventing misuse, and troubleshooting, where those interests do not override your rights.
  • Consent: only where consent is legally required for a particular processing activity. Device permission prompts let you control access to selected photos or the camera; they do not by themselves establish the legal basis for every related processing activity.
  • Legal obligation: where processing is necessary to comply with applicable law.

These legal bases are not yet a verified record of processing. The controller must confirm the purpose-by-purpose basis and whether any consent mechanism is needed before this Policy is published.

5. Who can receive information

  • Convex: provides the backend database and file storage. The configured Convex deployment region has been reported as EU (eu-west-1); confirm this in the deployment dashboard before publication.
  • Clerk: provides account authentication. Its configured data region has not been confirmed; the placeholder [Vendor Default / EU — confirm in Clerk dashboard] must be resolved. Clerk may process sign-in information and authentication identifiers to provide its service.
  • Google: if you choose Google OAuth, Google processes authentication data under its own terms and privacy policy. Google is an identity provider, not identified here as a Codexgram backend processor. See Google Privacy Policy and Google Terms.
  • Other users: information you choose to make visible through profiles, posts, comments, stories, likes, follows, and messages is shared as described in Section 6.

We do not currently integrate an advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or push-notification SDK. The operator reports Convex and Clerk as the only configured backend processors/subprocessors. Google is a separate identity provider when you choose Google sign-in. Confirm the current provider subprocessor lists and any web hosting provider before publication.

We may also disclose information if required by law or a valid legal request, or where reasonably necessary to protect users, the Service, or legal rights. This is a description of possible legal compliance, not a claim that such disclosures are routinely made.

6. Who can see your content

  • Profiles and posts are available to signed-in, active Codexgram members. Information in your profile and content may therefore be seen by members you do not personally know.
  • Stories are returned in the active-story feed to members who follow the author. They stop appearing as active after 24 hours, but this expiry does not itself delete the stored media file.
  • Direct messages are available in the app to the members of the conversation. They are not end-to-end encrypted. Do not send information that must remain confidential.
  • Media is served through Convex Storage URLs. A person who obtains a media URL may be able to access the associated file; do not assume that a link is private merely because it is not shown in a public profile.

Other members may copy, capture, or share content they can view. We cannot control copies made outside the Service.

8. Retention and deletion

We retain account information, profile data, content, and social records while needed to operate your account and the features you use. You can initiate account deletion from the profile screen. The app attempts to delete associated Convex records and files and then separately requests deletion of your Clerk account.

Deletion is not guaranteed to remove every copy immediately. File deletion is best-effort; the Clerk request can fail after Convex data has been deleted; and the app does not configure its own backup retention schedule. Convex and Clerk data, backups, and logs may remain according to [Vendor Default Retention Period] and applicable provider policies. The provider retention periods and deletion behavior have not been confirmed. Contact us if deletion reports an error.

A story ceasing to appear after 24 hours is not the same as deletion of its stored file. We do not currently provide an in-app control to export or download all account data.

9. Security and automated processing

The app connects Clerk authentication with Convex backend access. On native devices, the Clerk Expo token cache uses Expo SecureStore; the web build does not use that native token cache. These implementation details do not guarantee that every system, provider, or transmission is secure. No method of storage or transmission can be represented as risk-free.

We do not currently use AI to generate content, make decisions about users, or profile users for advertising. We have not identified automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects. Activity updates and feed behavior are app features, not an AI decision system.

10. Your choices and privacy rights

You can edit profile details, choose which content to submit, change the app language, sign out, or initiate account deletion in the app. You can deny photo-library or camera permissions in your device settings, although related features may then be unavailable.

If the GDPR or another privacy law applies, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data; restriction of or objection to certain processing; and portability where applicable. You may withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, without affecting processing already carried out. You may also complain to your relevant supervisory authority; in Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

To make a privacy request, email [contact@codexgram.app]. We may need to verify your identity before responding. No in-app data-access or export workflow is currently available. Applicable law may limit or condition a particular right.

11. Cookies and local storage

Codexgram does not currently configure advertising or product-analytics cookies or SDKs. The web sign-in experience is supplied by Clerk and may use essential cookies or browser storage for authentication and security. The precise browser storage behavior depends on Clerk’s web configuration and should be checked in the deployed site before publication.

On native devices, the selected language is stored locally using AsyncStorage, and Clerk uses the native SecureStore token cache described above. These settings are not an in-app advertising or analytics profile.

12. Children

Codexgram is intended only for invited adults aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly design the beta for children, and the app does not collect a date of birth or verify age. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us at the address below so we can review the request.
